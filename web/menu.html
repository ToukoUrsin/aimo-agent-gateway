Order from a menu

Take a photo of a menu, a lunch list or a week plan. A model reads it. Code works out what to buy, checks every line and makes one order. If a rule asks for a person, someone other than you approves it on their own device.

or pick a sample photo

The sample photos were made with an image model for this page. They are not a real kitchen's menus. Everything that happens after you pick one is real: the reading, the checks, the order.

Test control: make a product short

This pretends other customers reserved the stock of one product on your test account, for two hours. Valio Aimo's data is not changed. Plan a menu after it to see how the shortage is solved.
