Put it in your own Claude

The same endpoint is a custom connector. Claude draws these cards on the web, in the desktop app and on the phone; ChatGPT reads the same card. Signing in gives your assistant a mandate on a sandbox kitchen of your own, with limits you set on the consent page.

  1. In Claude: Customize, Connectors, Add custom connector. Name it and paste the address.
  2. Press Connect. Our consent page opens: choose what the assistant may do alone, then allow.
  3. Ask: “We need 1,000 litres of lactose-free milk by Monday. What are the options?”
  4. Open Claude with the connector filled in does step 1 for you. Other agents and the REST API: Connect your own assistant.
